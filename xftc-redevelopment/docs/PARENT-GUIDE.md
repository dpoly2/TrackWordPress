# Parent Guide

This is written for families using the site — feel free to share it directly with parents, or adapt it
into your own welcome email. It walks through registering, using the parent portal, and paying fees.

---

## Creating your account & registering your athlete

Go to your club's **Register** page. Registration is a short, four-step form:

1. **Parent / Guardian Account** — your name, email, and a password. This becomes your login for the
   parent portal.
2. **Athlete Information** — your athlete's name, date of birth, gender, school, team level, and an
   emergency contact.
3. **Season & Membership Tier** — pick the current season and choose **Standard** or **Premium**
   membership (Premium usually includes extras like invitational travel — check with your club for
   specifics).
4. **Review & Submit** — confirm everything, agree to the liability waiver and code of conduct, and
   consent to the club collecting your athlete's information (required — this covers what's described
   in the club's privacy policy).

Submitting the form creates your account, adds your athlete's profile, signs you up for the selected
season, and logs you straight into your **Parent Portal**.

Registering additional athletes (siblings) or additional seasons for the same athlete is done from
inside the portal once you're logged in.

---

## The Parent Portal

Once logged in, your portal has several tabs:

- **My Athletes** — your registered athletes at a glance.
- **Schedule** — upcoming meets, with a **Register** button to sign an athlete up for a specific meet
  and event.
- **Results** — your athlete's results history, with a performance chart per event. Personal bests are
  marked with a badge.
- **Payments** — your payment history and balance for membership/travel fees.
- **Travel** — any bus/hotel bookings for your athlete(s), and their payment status.
- **Store Orders** *(only shown if the club's merchandise store is active)* — your order history for
  uniforms/gear purchased through the site.

You can log back in any time from the **Parent Portal** page using the email and password you set at
registration.

---

## Paying fees

From the **Payments** tab (or wherever your club links to checkout), you'll be taken to a secure Stripe
checkout page to pay your membership or travel balance by card. You'll get an email receipt once the
payment goes through, and your balance updates automatically — no need to tell the club separately.

If you're paying by cash or check instead, let your club admin know — they can record that payment
manually and it'll show up in your portal the same way.

---

## Registering for a meet

From the **Schedule** tab, find the meet you want and click **Register**. If your club has enabled travel
booking for that meet, you'll also be able to reserve a bus seat and/or hotel room, and the associated
fee will be added to your balance.

You can only register your own athletes — the system checks that automatically.

---

## Your data & privacy

Because your athlete's information includes details about a minor, you have the right to request an
export or deletion of your family's data at any time — ask your club admin, who can run this through
WordPress's built-in Tools > Export/Erase Personal Data. Deleting an athlete's personal details keeps
their results/participation history in anonymized form for club records, but removes their name, date of
birth, school, and emergency contact.
