# Coach & Staff Guide

Coaches and staff don't need a WordPress Admin login to do their day-to-day work — everything below
happens on your club's regular website, through the **Staff/Coach Portal** page.

Ask your club admin for the portal page link and your login (they'll set up your account with the
**Coach** and/or **Staff** role).

---

## Logging in

Log in through your club's **Parent Portal** login form using the email/password your admin set up for
you, then go to the **Staff/Coach Portal** page. What you see there depends on your role — coaches get
meet tools, staff get pay history, and if you're both you'll see both sections.

---

## For coaches: entering results

The staff portal shows your club's upcoming and active meets. To record a result:

1. Pick the **Meet** from the dropdown.
2. Enter the athlete's **ID** (ask your admin if you don't have this handy, or look them up in the
   Members list if you have admin access).
3. Pick the **Event** and enter the **Result** (a time, distance, or points) and, if you have it, the
   **Placement**.
4. Click **Save Result**.

The system automatically checks whether that result is a new **personal best** for the athlete or a new
**club record**, and flags it — no extra step needed on your end. The athlete's family will see it on
their portal's Results tab with a badge.

---

## For staff: viewing your pay

If your account has the **Staff** role, the portal shows your pay history — hours, gross pay, net pay,
and status (Pending, Paid, or Voided) for each payroll period your admin has entered. This is read-only;
your admin enters hours and processes payroll from their side.

If you don't see a pay history section, it means your staff record hasn't been linked to your user
account yet — ask your admin to do that under **Xtreme Force > Payroll**.
